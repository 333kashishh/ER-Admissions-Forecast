Model details

Negative Binomial regression. The log of expected admissions is a weighted sum of the features; each weight (β) becomes a multiplier eβ.

log(μ) = 4.6815 + 0.2372·Heatwave + 0.1575·Coldwave − 0.0742·Tue − 0.1107·Wed − 0.1181·Thu − 0.1081·Fri − 0.2103·Sat − 0.2346·Sun + 0.000913·AQI + 0.1672·Flu + 0.0459·Trend

Dispersion α = 0.0167, so variance = μ + 0.0167·μ². Trend is set to 3.0 years (start of 2026). Staffing ratios are planning assumptions you can change above.
